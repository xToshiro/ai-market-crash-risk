### In-Depth Forensic Analysis of the 3 Scenarios:

### Scenario 1: "Orderly Deceleration & Multiple Compression" (Probability: 48%)
* **Mechanism:** Enterprise software buyers encounter diminishing returns from scaling larger language models (*The Wall of Scaling Laws*). Cloud hyperscalers freeze capex acceleration beyond committed purchase orders.
* **Asset Class Dynamics:**
  * The S&P 500 Forward P/E multiple compresses from **23.4x to 18.0x** (reverting to its 10-year historical average).
  * Sector rotation takes hold: capital rotates out of high-multiple semiconductor names and into defensive cash-generative sectors (commercial banks, regulated power utilities, healthcare, and consumer staples).
  * Senior (**Class A**) Data Center ABS notes maintain uninterrupted coupon and amortization payments, anchored by long-term traditional enterprise and cloud workloads.

### Scenario 2: "The AI Subprime Shock & Private Credit Stress" (Probability: 34%)
* **Mechanism:** Commercial deployment of Nvidia Blackwell and Rubin architectures crushes secondary market rental rates for H100 server clusters by over 65%. Generative AI startups burning $500M/month fail to raise follow-on venture rounds at prior step-ups and terminate compute reservations.
* **Asset Class Dynamics:**
  * Neoclouds (CoreWeave, Lambda) undergo forced debt restructurings with private credit syndicates. Pledged GPU collateral falls deeply underwater.
  * Private credit funds suspend investor redemptions in technology infrastructure strategies and record marked-to-market portfolio write-downs of 25% to 40%.
  * Public equities endure sharp drawdowns; Nvidia corrects violently to price in a multi-quarter hardware digestion cycle (analogous to the 2001 telecom downturn or 2018 crypto winter).
  * Structured Data Center ABS platforms trigger contractual **Cash Trap Events**; sponsor equity distributions are halted; mezzanine Class B notes suffer liquidity freezes and PIK conversions.

### Scenario 3: "The Systemic Perfect Storm" (Probability: 18%)
* **Mechanism:** The confluence of three synchronized structural crises:
  1. The unwinding of circular AI vendor financing;
  2. A failed auction concession in the US Treasury market, where a $40 Trillion national debt burden requires daily issuance of $10B+, pushing 10-year yields past **6.00%**;
  3. Refinancing maturities exceeding $1.5 Trillion in Commercial Real Estate (CRE) loans held on regional bank balance sheets.
* **Asset Class Dynamics:**
  * Disorderly liquidations across off-balance-sheet SPVs and private credit vehicles trigger contagion across life insurers and pension plans.
  * Equities experience a protracted two-year bear market (-50% to -60% across tech benchmarks).
  * The Federal Reserve is forced into emergency intervention via discount window easing and renewed Quantitative Easing (QE) to stabilize the sovereign bond market and restore banking liquidity.
